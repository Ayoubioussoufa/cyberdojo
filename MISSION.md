# Mission: go from zero to a job-ready defender

## Why
A Master's student in cybersecurity, starting from zero in practical skills, wants to become a junior professional who can prove what she can do, using the 24-month roadmap (Cybersecurity-Roadmap-24-Months.pdf).

## Success looks like
- She passes each month's exit gate unaided.
- 16 published projects plus a capstone, each with a report.
- A strong junior profile in one chosen track, with 2–3 targeted certifications.
- She can explain her work under questioning, and verify what AI tells her.

## Constraints
- 12–15 hours a week alongside a Master's. 5 hours a week in exam periods.
- She knows nothing yet, so nothing may be assumed. Every term is defined.
- English course. Morocco and francophone markets matter for careers.

## Out of scope
- Working exploits, payloads and evasion recipes, in any part of the course. Hands-on offensive practice happens on legal platforms such as PortSwigger, pwn.college and Hack The Box.
