## Modules

### Foundation

- [iptables and nftables](01-iptables-and-nftables.md): The Linux packet filter from the kernel's view. Netfilter hooks, connection tracking, rule order, NAT and logging, so you can write a firewall and read one under pressure.

### Intermediate

- [Writing IDS rules](02-ids-rule-writing.md): From reading a Suricata rule to writing and testing your own. Signature anatomy, performance, false positives, Snort and Suricata differences, and Zeek's different approach.
- [Sensors and TLS inspection](03-sensors-and-tls-inspection.md): Where to put network sensors, how TAPs differ from SPAN ports, what next-generation firewalls really add, and the honest trade-offs of breaking TLS to inspect it.
- [Network security architecture](04-network-architecture.md): From flat networks to designs that assume breach. Tiers, microsegmentation, bastions, 802.1X, egress control and DDoS protection, with the failure each one answers.
- [Honeypots and deception](05-deception.md): Instead of hunting for intruders among millions of normal events, plant something that no honest person will touch. Honeypots, honeytokens, canaries and how to run them safely.
- [VPN internals](06-vpn-internals.md): How IPsec, WireGuard and OpenVPN really work, why their designs differ so much, and why the VPN appliance at your edge is so often the way in.

### Advanced

- [Zero Trust in depth](07-zero-trust-in-depth.md): What Zero Trust really means once you strip away the marketing. NIST's model, policy engines, ZTNA, SASE and service meshes, plus how to get there in stages.
