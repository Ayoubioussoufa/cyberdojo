## Modules

### Foundation

- [Computer crime law](01-computer-crime-law.md): Where the legal lines sit for security work. Authorisation, the main laws in the US, UK, EU, France and Morocco, tools, hacking back, and how cross-border cases work.
- [Disclosure and professional ethics](02-disclosure-and-ethics.md): What to do when you find a flaw. Coordinated disclosure, bug bounties, safe harbour, handling sensitive data during tests, and the ethics that separate this field from crime.

### Intermediate

- [Quantitative risk with FAIR](03-quantitative-risk.md): Turn "high, medium, low" into ranges of money that a business can compare. FAIR's factors, calibrated estimates, Monte Carlo simulation, and how to avoid false precision.
- [NIST RMF and control catalogues](04-nist-rmf-and-controls.md): How a large organisation goes from "we should be secure" to named, tested controls. The Risk Management Framework, SP 800-53, control mapping, and how audits use them.
- [EU security regulation](05-eu-regulation.md): GDPR, NIS2, DORA, the Cyber Resilience Act and the AI Act. Who each one binds, what it demands, its deadlines, and why European rules reach companies outside Europe.
- [Sector compliance and vendor risk](06-sector-compliance.md): PCI DSS for card data, HIPAA for health data, and how to judge a supplier's security. Scope reduction, SOC 2 reports, and why your vendors are part of your attack surface.
- [Security metrics and board reporting](07-metrics-and-reporting.md): Measure what changes a decision. Choosing useful metrics, avoiding vanity numbers, and telling a board what it needs to hear, including SEC disclosure rules.
