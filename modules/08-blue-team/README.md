## Modules

### Foundation

- [SIEM platforms and query languages](01-siem-query-languages.md): The same question in Splunk SPL, Microsoft KQL and Elastic ES|QL. How data models make queries portable, and how to think in pipelines instead of memorising syntax.
- [Tabletop exercises](04-tabletop-exercises.md): Rehearse a crisis while nothing's on fire. How to design, run and learn from a tabletop exercise, so that the first time your team makes these decisions isn't during a real incident.

### Intermediate

- [Threat hunting](02-threat-hunting.md): Looking for what your alerts missed. Hypotheses, the long tail, beacon statistics, and how every hunt should leave your detection stronger than it found it.
- [Purple teaming](03-purple-teaming.md): Prove that your detections work, instead of hoping. How red and blue collaborate, how Atomic Red Team and Caldera test coverage, and how to turn results into honest metrics.
- [Disk forensics](05-disk-forensics.md): Reconstructing what happened from a disk image. NTFS internals, timestamps that lie, deleted files, the Sleuth Kit and Autopsy, and working so that your findings stand up.
- [Linux forensics](07-linux-forensics.md): Most servers run Linux, yet most forensics training is about Windows. Where Linux keeps its evidence, how to collect it, and how to build a timeline from logs, shells and files.

### Advanced

- [Memory forensics](06-memory-forensics.md): RAM holds what disks never see. Acquiring memory safely, how Volatility rebuilds processes and connections, and how to find injected code and hidden activity.
- [Network and cloud forensics](08-network-and-cloud-forensics.md): Investigating what crossed the wire, and what happened on servers you can't touch. Reconstructing sessions from captures and flows, plus evidence in AWS, Azure and GCP.
- [Anti-forensics and reporting](09-anti-forensics-and-reporting.md): How investigators recognise tampering with evidence, what silence in the logs tells you, and how to write forensic findings that hold up under cross-examination.
