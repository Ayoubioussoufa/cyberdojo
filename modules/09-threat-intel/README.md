### Foundation

- [Attack models compared](01-attack-models.md): Kill Chain, Unified Kill Chain, ATT&CK, D3FEND, CAPEC and the Diamond Model. What each one is for, how they fit, and how a defender actually uses them.

### Intermediate

- [Threat modelling methods](02-threat-modelling-methods.md): Beyond STRIDE. DREAD and why it faded, attack trees, PASTA, LINDDUN and lightweight approaches, so that you can pick a method that fits the system and the team.
- [Threat actors and attribution](03-threat-actors.md): Who attacks, why, and how their businesses work. States, criminals and insiders, the ransomware economy, how groups get named, and why attribution is so hard.
- [Sharing threat intelligence](04-cti-sharing.md): STIX, TAXII, OpenCTI and information sharing communities. How to move intelligence between tools, judge a feed's quality, and turn reports into something a SOC can use.
- [OSINT and your attack surface](05-osint-and-recon.md): What the Internet already says about you. Open-source intelligence, passive reconnaissance, and how defenders use the same sources to find exposure before an attacker does.
