## Modules

### Foundation

- [Linux processes and /proc](01-linux-processes-and-proc.md): How a process really starts, what fork and exec do, why everything is a file descriptor, and how /proc and strace let you see a running program from the outside.

### Intermediate

- [Linux boot, linking and login](02-linux-boot-and-linking.md): What runs before your shell does. The boot chain, systemd, the dynamic linker, PAM and password storage, and why each is a favourite place to hide.
- [Linux confinement](03-linux-confinement.md): Beyond rwx. ACLs, capabilities, SELinux, AppArmor, seccomp, namespaces and cgroups. These are the building blocks of every container, and of every serious hardening guide.
- [Linux privilege escalation](04-linux-privilege-escalation.md): How a low-privileged account becomes root, explained as one recurring pattern, and how to audit, harden and detect each path before someone else finds it.
- [Windows tokens and privileges](06-windows-tokens-and-privileges.md): Every Windows security decision starts with a token. How tokens, privileges and integrity levels work, why service accounts are a gift, and what UAC truly is.
- [Windows credential stores](07-windows-credential-stores.md): Where Windows keeps secrets, and why one compromised laptop can give up a domain. LSASS, SAM, DPAPI and NTDS.dit, then Credential Guard and how to detect theft.
- [Windows privilege escalation](08-windows-privilege-escalation.md): From user to SYSTEM on Windows. Services, DLL search order, installers and tasks, seen through one pattern, along with the audits and logs that close each path.

### Advanced

- [Linux persistence and audit](05-linux-persistence-and-audit.md): Where attackers stay on a Linux host, and how auditd and eBPF let you see it. From authorized_keys to kernel modules, with the detection for each.
- [Windows built-in defences](09-windows-defences.md): AMSI, ETW, App Control, AppLocker and Attack Surface Reduction. What each one sees, where it sits, and why tampering with them is itself one of your best detections.
- [Virtualisation and boot integrity](14-virtualisation-and-boot-integrity.md): What hypervisors isolate, and what they don't. Secure Boot, measured boot, the TPM and full-disk encryption, and how they combine to protect a machine from power-on.
