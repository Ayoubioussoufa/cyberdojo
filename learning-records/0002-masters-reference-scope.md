# Master's-level reference, defender's-side offence

The requester widened the mission on 7 Nov 2026: alongside the roadmap, `modules/` becomes a reference covering the whole field, including binary security, hardware, OT and mobile. Attack-heavy topics are taught from the defender's side (root causes, mitigations, detection), because attack-mechanics writing was stopped by a safety classifier twice. Hands-on offensive practice stays on legal platforms, so later teaching should point there and not fill the gap with more attack detail.
